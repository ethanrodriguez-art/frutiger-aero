What is Frutiger Aero?

Frutiger Aero is a design aesthetic that took over Technology, User Interfaces (UI), and Graphical Design

The design style was created by Adrian Frutiger. And then Windows Aero style was introduced in Windows Vista by 2006, which had translucent glass borders and glossy effects on applications and other parts of the UI.

Core Characteristics

  • Skeuomorphism
  • Glossy and glass textures
  • Nature images
  • Bright color palettes
  • Atmospheric effects

These are the core design characteristics of the Frutiger Aero style. Meant to show a optimistic, upbeat look into the future of technology. Where high technology and the natural world living in harmony.
